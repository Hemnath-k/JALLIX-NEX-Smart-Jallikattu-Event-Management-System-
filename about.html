About JALLIX-NEX

Preserving tradition through technology - Revolutionizing Jallikattu management

Our Mission

To modernize and streamline the organization, registration, and management of Jallikattu events while preserving the cultural significance and traditional values of this ancient sport. We aim to bring transparency, efficiency, and accessibility to all stakeholders involved.

Our Vision

To create a comprehensive digital ecosystem for traditional sports that bridges the gap between heritage and technology, ensuring the preservation and growth of Jallikattu for future generations while maintaining the highest standards of safety and animal welfare.

Our Values

Cultural Preservation, Technological Innovation, Transparency, Safety First, Community Engagement, and Sustainable Practices. We believe in honoring tradition while embracing progress.

Our Story

Bridging Tradition and Technology

JALLIX-NEX was born from a deep appreciation for the cultural heritage of Jallikattu and a recognition of the challenges faced in organizing and managing these traditional events. Founded in 2023 by a team of technology enthusiasts and cultural preservation advocates, our platform represents a harmonious blend of ancient tradition and modern innovation.

We noticed that while Jallikattu has deep roots in Tamil culture spanning thousands of years, the administrative processes surrounding these events remained largely manual and inefficient. Bull owners, tamers, and organizers faced challenges with registration, documentation, payment processing, and event coordination.

Our solution brings together all stakeholders on a single, secure platform that respects tradition while leveraging technology to enhance safety, transparency, and efficiency. We work closely with cultural experts, veterinary professionals, and community leaders to ensure our platform aligns with traditional values while meeting modern standards.
